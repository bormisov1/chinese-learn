Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

What we store

Hanzi Deck stores your study data — vocabulary, sentences, spaced-repetition progress, and review history — on our server so it can sync across your devices. Your study data is associated with your account.

Accounts

You can sign in with Google or Telegram. We store only the identity fields those providers return (such as your name, email address or Telegram ID, and avatar) to create and recognize your account. We never see your provider password.

Your AI API key and chat

The app's AI features (explanations and chat) call the AI provider directly from your device using an API key that you supply. That key is stored only on your device and is never sent to our server or included in backups. The text you send through AI features is processed by the AI provider under their privacy policy; prompts may include words and sentences from your study data.

What we don't do

We don't sell or share your data, we don't show ads, and we don't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Backups

Backups you export are JSON files that contain your study data but never your API key. They are handled entirely by you.

Deletion

You can delete your study data from the app at any time, or contact us to delete your account and all associated data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the app or to the site owner via zh.x.bormisov.com.
