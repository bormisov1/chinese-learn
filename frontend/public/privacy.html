Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

What we store

Hanzi Deck stores your study data — vocabulary, sentences, spaced-repetition progress, and review history — on our server so it can sync across your devices. Your study data is associated with your account.

Accounts

You can sign in with Google or Telegram. We store only the identity fields those providers return (such as your name, email address or Telegram ID, and avatar) to create and recognize your account. We never see your provider password.

Your AI API key and AI-generated content

The app's optional AI features call DeepSeek directly from your device using an API key that you supply. That key is stored only on your device and is never sent to our server or included in backups. When you request these features, DeepSeek processes prompts that may contain words, sentences, answers, or chat messages from your study data and generates practice sentences and translations, answer evaluations, corrections and feedback, word and sentence explanations, missing-word translations, chat replies, chat titles, and word suggestions. AI output may be inaccurate. DeepSeek processes this data under its privacy policy.

Handwriting and photo recognition

On native apps, Google ML Kit recognizes handwriting and Chinese text from photos on your device. Your strokes, images, recognized text, and recognition results are not sent to Google by ML Kit. ML Kit may contact Google to download models and updates and sends device and app information, per-installation identifiers, configured languages, performance and usage metrics, feature sizes and versions, event types, and error codes to Google for diagnostics, analytics, maintenance, improvement, and abuse prevention. See ML Kit Terms & Privacy. Web recognition also runs locally using HanziLookup and Tesseract.js; Tesseract language models are downloaded from a content-delivery network and cached by the browser.

Purchases and subscriptions

RevenueCat processes purchase and subscription status for Hanzi Deck. RevenueCat and the store or payment provider (such as Apple, Google, or the web payment processor) may receive an app user identifier, product and transaction details, subscription status, device and app information, and diagnostics needed to complete purchases and restore access. We use this information only to provide and manage premium access. See RevenueCat's privacy policy and your store or payment provider's privacy policy for details.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal data, show ads, or use advertising trackers. Data is disclosed to service providers only as needed for account sync, AI requests you initiate, purchase processing, and the technical ML Kit metrics described above.

Backups

Backups you export are JSON files that contain your study data but never your API key. They are handled entirely by you.

Deletion

You can delete your study data from the app at any time, or contact us to delete your account and all associated data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the app or to the site owner via zh.x.bormisov.com.

Licenses and credits
